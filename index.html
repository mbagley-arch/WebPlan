GRADE 9 • WEB DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT

MY WEBSITE BLUEPRINT

Three-Page Website Planning Project

Plan it. Sketch it. Organize it. Build it.

Project Overview

Your task is to plan a website containing three connected pages about a topic of your choice. Before writing HTML or CSS, you will decide what your website is about, who will use it, what each page will contain, and how visitors will navigate the site.

Learning goal: Create a clear website plan that demonstrates understanding of purpose, audience, navigation, layout, readability, usability, and accessibility.

Section 1: Purpose and Audience

Purpose is the reason a website exists. Audience is the group of people the website is designed to serve.

Will your website inform, teach, entertain, persuade, or help people?

Section 2: Plan Your Three Pages

Your three pages should have different purposes, useful content, and consistent navigation. You may change the suggested page names to match your topic.

Page 1: Home

Introduce your website and explain what visitors will find.

Page 2: About / Information

Explain your topic in greater detail and give visitors useful information.

Page 3: Explore / Resources

Give visitors another way to explore the topic.

Section 3: Create Your Sitemap

A sitemap shows how pages are organized and connected. Plan a consistent navigation menu so visitors can reach all three pages.

Section 4: Sketch Your Homepage Wireframe

A wireframe is a simple layout plan that shows where the main parts of a page will go. It focuses on structure rather than finished colors or images.

Your design can be different. The purpose is to organize content before you build the website.

Section 5: Choose Your Visual Design

Your design should be attractive, consistent, readable, and appropriate for your audience.

Section 6: Usability and Accessibility

Check each item when you have planned how your website will meet the requirement.

Remember: Accessibility means designing a website so people with different abilities can use it. Usability means visitors can complete their tasks easily. A good website needs both.

Section 7: Final Reflection

Final Submission Checklist

Before submitting your planning template, make sure you have:

  • Identified your website's purpose and target audience.
  • Planned content for all three pages.
  • Created a sitemap and navigation plan.
  • Sketched or described your homepage wireframe.
  • Selected colors, fonts, and an image style.
  • Reviewed the usability and accessibility checklist.
  • Completed your reflection questions.
